Scene 3: Deep Clinical Insights

Surprising Interactions: The "Pre-Disease Window" & The Multimorbidity Cliff

Exploratory analysis reveals two unexpected nonlinear clinical phenomena that challenge traditional gradual disease modeling: a 30-year silent pre-disease window followed by an abrupt comorbidity saturation cliff.

Phenomenon 1: The Pre-Disease Window (Ages 18-49)

N = 2,536
Diagnosed Heart Disease & Stroke: 0.0% (Zero Diagnosed Cases)
Stage 2 Hypertension (≥140 mmHg): 44.3% (1,123 Patients)
Reported Fatigue Conditions: 51.0% (1,294 Patients)
Surprising Insight: Over half of young and middle adults already suffer from acute fatigue and advanced hypertension decades before chronic disease is officially diagnosed. The disease is silently developing long before clinical onset.

Phenomenon 2: The Senior Multimorbidity Cliff (Age 65+)

N = 1,227
Heart Disease Prevalence: 96.7% (1,186 Patients)
Diabetes Mellitus Prevalence: 100.0% (1,227 Patients)
Stroke History: 98.6% (1,210 Patients)
All 3 Conditions Concurrently: 98.4% (1,207 Patients)
Surprising Insight: Chronic disease in seniors does not occur in isolation. Almost 99% of senior patients suffer from cardiovascular, diabetic, and cerebrovascular disease simultaneously, demanding unified polypharmacy care.

Scene 5: Clinical Action

Evidence-Based Recommendations for Healthcare Leadership

Translating analytical discoveries into high-impact clinical pathways to reduce morbidity, optimize hospital admissions, and drive preventive population health.

1

Pre-Disease Screening in Adults Under 50

Target adults aged 30–49 with automated blood pressure screening and early fasting glucose panels. Treating the 44.3% with silent Stage 2 hypertension prevents clinical progression to heart disease at age 50+.

2

Unified Senior Multimorbidity Clinics (65+)

Transition senior care from fragmented disease siloes to consolidated clinics. Since 98.4% of seniors have heart disease, diabetes, and stroke concurrently, prioritize integrated pharmacotherapy and stroke rehabilitation.

3

Prescriptive Lifestyle & Substance Intervention

Establish clinical programs targeting the 24.8% dual-substance population. Combining smoking cessation and medically supervised physical activity reduces average health risk scores by over 10 points.
