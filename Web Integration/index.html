Integrated Visualization Architecture

The embedded Tableau solution combines 9 distinct analytical components:

1. KPI Banner Patient Health & Disease Overview metrics providing institutional baseline.
2. Bar Chart: Blood Pressure Average Blood Pressure Across Age Groups with 130 mmHg AHA Stage 1 line.
3. Grouped Bar Chart: Substances Alcohol & Smoking Consumption distribution across all 6 age cohorts (~50%).
4. Donut Chart: Exercise Physical Activity Distribution: Low (32.9%), Moderate (33.5%), High (33.5%).
5. Pie Chart: Age Distribution Patient Distribution by Age Group showing balanced life-stage volumes.
6. Grouped Bar: Disease by BMI Heart Disease vs. Diabetes Prevalence comparing metabolic vs. adiposity risks.
7. Bar Chart: BMI vs. Heart Direct step-ladder escalation from Underweight (20.5%) up to Obese (40.0%).
8. Heat Map: Risk Matrix Heart Disease by BMI & Exercise, illustrating the exercise protective buffer.
9. Bar Chart: Fatigue Condition Distribution by Fatigue Condition across age groups (~51% baseline marker).
